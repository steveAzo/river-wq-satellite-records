\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study changed the question for river water-quality telemetry over a one-way satellite link from how to deliver the whole record to what the server can soundly decide from what arrives. A certificate language turns each piece of received information into an interval and answers threshold, extremum, conductance-change, and rolling-mean queries with exceeded, not exceeded, or undetermined; the answers are sound when the intervals are valid, exact only without witnesses, and limited by the tolerance for readings that lie near a threshold.

In a pre-registered confirmation on 31 sealed rivers, eight of nine hypotheses held, although some of the eight could not fail by design (H1 through assumption R, H7, and most of H9) and the informative ones are H2 to H5, the unanticipated part of H9, and H8. No certified decision was wrong. Targeted witnesses raised the share of decided hard cases over a record alone by 0.30 to 0.32 at six messages per day and 30\% loss, and decided thresholds that nobody configured. Decision flags remained better for thresholds fixed at configuration time, but they decide from the flags alone; a hybrid that hosts witnesses in the spare slots of the flag design cost nothing on configured thresholds and decided 0.08 to 0.10 more hard cases than the record alone, and 0.29 to 0.36 more than the flags, on unanticipated thresholds. The hypothesis on false certificates after a forged message that passes the 16-bit check failed at twelve messages per day, and the failure points to days on which no message can be compared, which a longer check does not remove.

The claim of this study is generality and soundness, not the best decisions for known thresholds, and the evidence is trace-driven simulation. Three steps would move it toward practice: a measurement of real channel loss and error statistics, a receiver design whose integrity is measured as an unconditional rate at a stated bit-error rate, with a record-level check and a longer per-message check, tested on a new cohort, and an outside review of the code and proofs. The software, the frozen plan, and every result file are in the project repository.
